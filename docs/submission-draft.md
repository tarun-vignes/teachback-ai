# Teachback submission draft

## Pitch

Teachback turns “I think I understand” into a short cycle of explanation, source-linked feedback, practice, and reflection. Students paste or import their notes, explain a concept, and receive feedback grounded in those notes. A downloadable learning receipt preserves their original and revised explanations.

## Problem and audience

Students can reread familiar material without noticing gaps in their explanations. Teachback gives them a focused way to inspect those gaps using their own study material.

## AI implementation

Gemini compares explanations with supplied notes, returns structured findings and quoted passages, asks a practice question, and assesses the revised explanation. Server validation checks response shape and quote presence. Unsupported findings become uncertain. The app never treats a matching quote as proof of a correct interpretation.

## Technologies

Node.js, browser JavaScript, HTML/CSS, Mozilla PDF.js, and Google Gemini generateContent with structured output.
