## What to build

Build a functional AI-powered application that solves a clear real-world problem or provides a useful experience. Beginner-level implementations are welcome; usefulness and understanding matter more than complexity.

Suggested areas:

1. **AI chatbots:** answer questions or help with everyday problems.
2. **Document Q&A:** answer questions about uploaded notes, PDFs, or study materials.
3. **AI study assistants:** explain concepts, summarize notes, generate quizzes, or support exam preparation.
4. **AI productivity tools:** organize information, manage tasks, or automate repetitive work.
5. **AI for social good:** address community, education, accessibility, or everyday challenges.

Other AI application ideas are welcome if they meet the event requirements. Free tools, open-source models, and free-tier services are encouraged; paid software or API credits are not required. Check service limits and terms before choosing a provider.
